# Privacy Policy

TabAddict operates entirely on your local device.

**What TabAddict accesses:** TabAddict reads the URLs of your open browser
tabs when you click Capture. This data is used only to build a session
snapshot in the editor and is never transmitted anywhere.

**What TabAddict stores:** Session text you choose to save can be stored
locally in your browser using `chrome.storage.local`. It never leaves your
device.

**What TabAddict does not do:** TabAddict collects no analytics, contacts no
external servers, and shares no data with any third party — ever.

For questions or concerns, open an issue at https://github.com/peterthenelson/tabAddict.
